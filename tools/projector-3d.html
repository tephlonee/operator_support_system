Assumptions and limits

  • Mounting position, crane size and the line budget are assumptions, not measured at Brålanda.
  • A real laser projector must be calibrated on fixed reference points, e.g. the sheet's corner stops on the table.
  • Lines are well visible on black steel, less so on polished aluminium or stainless steel.
  • Industrial outline projectors are typically laser class 2 (do not stare into the beam).
